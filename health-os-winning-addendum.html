OpenAI Build Week · Apps for Your Life · Winning scope

Evidence to action—not another health dashboard.

The winning product is the complete evidence-to-action system: first make the source data tangible in Observe, then discover a pattern, replay it, challenge it, explain it, test a change, learn from the result, and preserve a trustworthy personal health history. Observe is an invitation into the evidence loop—not the final product.

Open the canonical build plan
Verdict: this is the pitch to build and submit. “Health + calendar + AI” is not novel by itself. The defensible product is the closed evidence lifecycle. It gives judges one human story, one visible technical idea, and one useful outcome.

Why Observe earns a place in the winning scope

Instant orientation

In seconds, users can see whether WhyPulse has heart, sleep, activity, recurring-event, and manual context—not just a mysterious “connected” badge.

A strong opening visual

The 30-day data rhythm and switchable signal chart make the invisible data spine feel polished before the Moment Fingerprint reveal.

Trust before AI

Exact local counts, honest gaps, Live/Demo labels, and identity-free Calendar rows show that the product knows what it has and what it does not.

Leads somewhere useful

Observe flows into Today and Evidence. It never competes with the core loop by inventing wellness scores, generic advice, or a second set of insights.

Winning-demo rule: spend roughly 10–12 seconds here. Establish visual quality and data trust, then move to the repeated-pattern story where WhyPulse differentiates.

If a feature cannot answer “which pitch stage does this improve?” it is not core. The full implementation details, model limits, source rules, and build gates live only in the canonical plan.

CriterionWhyPulse proof

Final recommendation Build the seven-stage loop deeply on Android. Use Health Connect, Android Calendar, Manual Check-ins, and Demo Data. Open with a brief, beautiful Observe overview, then keep the demo focused on the recurring-meeting Moment Fingerprint. Do not spend the pitch on connector breadth, speculative twins, model training, or separate screens for concepts that belong inside Evidence or History.
