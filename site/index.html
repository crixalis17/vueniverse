02 Bounded MedGemma

AI explains only what passed.

MedGemma can turn the calculated evidence into plain language on device. An output guard checks every answer; safe reviewed wording takes over if the model is unavailable or invalid.

  • Evidence-grounded explanations
  • No diagnosis or treatment answers
  • Checked fallback always available

Privacy by architecture

Your universe stays yours.

Personal records live in an encrypted, app-private local database. Vueniverse reduces sensitive context before analysis and keeps real and fictional data in physically separate stores.
