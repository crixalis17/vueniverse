## Expanded app recording choreography

Record the 1:55 app section as one human-paced session when practical. Hard
cuts are allowed at the named screen transitions, but never replace movement
with screenshots. Enable Android touch indicators before recording.

### 1. Landing and start — 13 seconds

1. Begin with the landing screen fully settled.
2. Hold the brand and privacy line for two seconds.
3. Use one short, slow swipe so the three-step product loop moves naturally.
4. Tap **See how it works**, hold both start paths, then tap
   **Explore Snapshot**.
5. Leave roughly one second for Today to settle.

### 2. Today — 10 seconds

1. Hold `Snapshot is ready · 30 days loaded · encrypted locally`.
2. Slowly expose the supported recurring 1:1 card.
3. Pause on `+11 bpm`, `6 of 8`, and data availability.
4. Return to the source-data control and tap it visibly.

### 3. Observe — 14 seconds

1. Hold the top card for five seconds so the record count and encryption line
   are readable.
2. Swipe once over 1.5–2 seconds; do not fling.
3. Pause on the signal chart and events lane.
4. Navigate back with a visible touch.

### 4. Pattern — 16 seconds

1. Open the supported card and hold all three hero metrics for five seconds.
2. Slowly move to the comparison chart.
3. Pause on meeting traces and the matched baseline.
4. Tap **Review the data**.

### 5. Evidence — 16 seconds

1. Use two deliberate swipes lasting roughly 1.5 seconds each.
2. Pause once on the numeric cards and once on comparison rules.
3. Ensure counterexamples and exclusions are readable before tapping
   **Explain this pattern**.

### 6. MedGemma — 16 seconds

1. Reset Snapshot before the take so no cached explanation is reused.
2. Confirm loopback health and readiness plus ADB reverse on port `8765`.
3. Preserve **4–5 seconds** of the real progress state.
4. Never stretch, freeze, or fabricate this wait in editing.
5. After completion, hold the explanation briefly and slowly center the
   runtime receipt.
6. Reject the take if it shows `Plain-language backup explanation` instead of
   the model and matching-evidence receipt.

### 7. Test, measured result, and proof — 30 seconds

1. Use visible back touches to return to Today.
2. Open the guided Snapshot and the quiet-buffer protocol.
3. Hold the protocol for at least four seconds.
4. Return and open the completed result; hold it for at least seven seconds.
5. Return and open Proof & Export; hold the receipt for the final eight
   seconds.
