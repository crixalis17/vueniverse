## Visual language for the illustrated pitch

Use off-white paper, a black marker, and only three project colors: lime for
evidence, cyan for data, and coral for uncertainty/exclusions. Keep the same
hand-drawn heart, calendar block, phone, and shield throughout so the drawings
feel like one continuous thought.

The creative transition at 0:58 is a match cut: the hand draws a phone outline
around the final diagram, then the paper phone becomes the real emulator at
1:00.

## Exact app journey for recording each clip

Record the app section as a continuous, human-paced navigation take with touch
indicators enabled. Trim only loading pauses and use hard cuts at the explicit
screen transitions above; never replace interaction with still screenshots.

### Clip A — Observe, 7 seconds

1. **Settings → Reset Snapshot**.
2. Return to **Today** and confirm `Snapshot is ready`, `30 days loaded`, and `encrypted locally`.
3. Tap **View source data**.
4. Begin recording with **Observe** already settled at the top.
5. Hold all seven seconds; do not scroll past the encryption line.

Required visible proof: `30-DAY SNAPSHOT`, `2,990 local records`,
`8 streams`, `30/30 days`, `SQLCipher-encrypted`, and
`separate Android Keystore key`.

### Clip B — Moment Fingerprint, 11 seconds

1. From **Today**, tap the supported recurring 1:1 card.
2. Begin recording after **Pattern detail** has loaded.
3. Hold the three hero metrics for 4 seconds.
4. Slowly swipe until the chart is centered.
5. Hold the chart for 4 seconds.

Required visible proof: `+11 bpm`, `6 of 8`, `42 min`, eight meeting traces,
and matched baseline.

### Clip C — Evidence, 11 seconds

1. On **Moment Fingerprint**, scroll down and tap **Review the data**.
2. Begin at the top of **Data behind the pattern**.
3. Use one smooth, slow scroll through the three metric cards.
4. End on comparison rules and **Which meetings did not match?**

Required visible proof: 12 checked, 8 compared, 6 positive, 2
counterexamples, four exclusions, `+11 bpm`, and `8–14 bpm`.

### Clip D — genuine loopback development inference, 14 seconds

1. Reset Snapshot immediately before the final take so no cached explanation is
   reused.
2. Confirm the local service is `ok` and `ready`, and ADB reverse for port
   `8765` is present.
3. Navigate back to **Evidence** and stop on **Explain this pattern**.
4. Start recording, tap the button, and preserve 2–4 seconds of genuine wait.
5. Show citations and uncertainty, then scroll to the receipt.

The receipt must show:

- `Local development machine · loopback service`
- `MedGemma 1.5 4B`
- a measured inference time
- `Model answer matched the current data`

If the receipt says **Plain-language backup explanation**, stop the recording.
Restore the local service/ADB bridge and run a fresh explanation. Never call
backup text model inference.

### Clip E — experiment setup, 5 seconds

1. Open **Run the guided recording** from **Today**.
2. Use **Review Test This** to open the quiet-buffer protocol.
3. Stage the top so `10 quiet minutes`, `3 eligible meetings`, and the
   stop-any-time boundary fit together.
4. Record five seconds without accepting consent or starting a new experiment.

### Clip F — completed experiment result, 7 seconds

1. From **Run the guided recording**, tap **Open completed result**.
2. Start with `STRENGTHENED · SNAPSHOT` and
   `Recovery was 9 minutes faster` visible.
3. Make one short swipe if needed to center the result card.
4. Hold `3 of 3`, `54 min`, `45 min`, and `9 min faster`.

### Clip G — proof and export, 3 seconds

1. From **Run the guided recording**, tap **Open Proof & Export**.
2. Stage `SNAPSHOT RECEIPT`, result version, file fingerprint, and the export
   control.
3. Record three seconds with no scrolling.

are not part of the final cut. The completed experiment earns those seconds
because it closes the product's central loop: observe, explain, test, improve.

lengthen the video.

## Recording and editing rules

- Record at 1080p and crop to the app or illustration canvas. Hide the emulator
  toolbar, desktop, terminals, account names, and notifications.
- Use hard cuts during the app demo. Use only the paper-to-phone match cut for
  a stylized transition.
- Keep UI text readable; do not accelerate app clips beyond 1.15×.
- The narration must be audible throughout. No copyrighted music is needed.
- Do not show a Google, OpenAI, Android, YouTube, or other third-party logo. Use
  plain-text product names only where needed to identify the required build
  tools or the actual runtime. Do not treat factual use as automatic permission;
  clear every visible or spoken third-party word mark before upload.
- Never imply that the recorded MedGemma response is phone-local. It is genuine
  inference from the loopback development service running on the development
  machine.
- Call the preloaded journey `Snapshot` consistently. Do not call Snapshot
  records real patient data or Live data.
- Keep at least one medical boundary visible: `pattern, not a medical
  conclusion` or `personal test, not treatment`.
- Export at **1:58** if possible. Reject any render at or above **2:00**.
